# Targeted evidence search — 16 November 2026

This is a focused primary-source investigation, not an exhaustive systematic
review. Searches targeted strong classical comparisons, data constraints,
regularization controls, quantum-memory/acquisition benefits and accessible
reproduction artifacts. Preprints were not admitted as standalone success evidence.

Author source trees were fetched using public GitHub metadata and pinned before
inspection. Exact commits and inspected-file hashes are retained. The original
papers and author code were not redistributed; the new native references were
written from the documented matrix/measurement contracts.

The expansion also audited the older non-winning local studies. Their observations
are scoped to recorded data/configurations/budgets in `experiment_outcomes.json`;
alternative training and data geometry are prospective hypotheses. None of the
old results was changed or removed. New paper protocols are plans, not new runs.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No statement that every successful paper identifies sufficient data conditions
  for beating classical methods on a new project.
- No invented numerical values extracted visually from continual-learning curves.
- No proof of causal quantum necessity from a numerical win over selected models.
- No local reproduction claim for CIFAR, graphon or photonic paper-scale results.
- No public release, source visibility change or replacement of the old reports.
